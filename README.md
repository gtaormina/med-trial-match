# med-trial-match — demo

Static demo of a study project: a first reading of Italian clinical notes to pre-screen
patients for clinical trials, with pseudonymisation before any LLM call, answers checked
against the record in code, and a person who decides.

**Live:** https://gtaormina.github.io/med-trial-match/

- **Not a medical device.** Research and study prototype; not for diagnosis, treatment or
  any decision about a person.
- **Invented patients only.** No real personal data anywhere. Trial texts come from
  ClinicalTrials.gov (public registry), with no link to their sponsors.
- The pages replay recorded runs: no server, no model calls, nothing is collected.

This repository holds only the built pages of the demo.
