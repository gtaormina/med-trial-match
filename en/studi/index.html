Pre-screening for clinical research

Who could join a clinical trial?

For four real trials registered on ClinicalTrials.gov, the system reads the record of invented patients: the structured data extracted from their notes, and the notes themselves. Each criterion becomes met, not met or to check, always with its evidence quoted word for word; a verifier written in code throws out any citation it cannot find. It is a pre-screening: the investigator decides.

As in real life, most patients do not fit a given trial: the useful work is finding the few worth a look, fast.
