# Trials used in this repository

Data from **ClinicalTrials.gov**, U.S. National Library of Medicine
(<https://clinicaltrials.gov>), retrieved through the API v2 (version 2.0.5) on the date
recorded in each `*.criteria.json` (`source_retrieved`); each study's own processing date is
in its `*.study.json`.

## Modifications made to the data (as the terms below require)

- `<NCT>.study.json` keeps only the fields this project reads (identification, conditions,
  eligibility, location countries) from the full API record.
- `<NCT>.criteria.json` splits the eligibility text into single criteria and adds an Italian
  rendering written by a language model, plus typed rules (age, sex, codes, thresholds)
  produced by a model, coded against this project's vocabulary and **checked by hand**
  (`reviewed: true`). The English text of each criterion is the registry's wording with
  formatting normalised: list markers and sub-item numbers dropped, line breaks changed, and
  markdown escapes (`\<`, `\&`, `\^`) removed in some criteria; no word is changed
  (`tests/test_trials.py::test_criterion_texts_are_faithful_to_the_registry`). Split
  sub-bullets were merged back into their criterion.
- `source_retrieved` is the date of the API data timestamp; `reports/trials.md` shows, for
  each trial, the date the data were processed by ClinicalTrials.gov and the record's last
  update.
- The files are a snapshot, not kept current: a trial may have changed or closed since the
  retrieval date. They are used as fixed test data for a portfolio project, never to inform
  anyone about trial availability.

## Corrections made by hand (2026-10-27)

- NCT07351058: BMI unit `kg/m^2` written as `kg/m2`; type 1 diabetes had been coded as type 2
  (44054006): rule removed; screening glucose values (a future measurement) and the weight
  *change* (read as a weight) left without rules; two lines of one bullet merged;
  cardiovascular events in 3 months: myocardial infarction or stroke within 90 days, other
  events not coded (not exhaustive).
- NCT07624305: "CKD and at least one of the following" merged with its two sub-items and
  encoded as two alternatives (eGFR 15–45 with albuminuria, the albuminuria part not coded;
  eGFR 15–30 with UACR < 30); the parser had coded CKD stage 1 only, now stages 1–4, end-stage
  and diabetic CKD; K+ also from whole blood (6298-4); UACR ≥ 5000 kept alone because UPCR is
  not in the vocabulary (not exhaustive); SBP > 180 or DBP > 110 as two alternatives;
  type 1 diabetes had been coded as type 2: rule removed and its sub-items merged; acute
  coronary syndrome had been coded as acute respiratory distress syndrome: replaced by
  myocardial infarction or stroke within 84 days (not exhaustive); transplant: history of renal
  or bone marrow transplant (not exhaustive); the medication list merged into one criterion.
- NCT07784725: the weight *change* left without a rule; "BMI ≥ 30, or ≥ 27 with a
  comorbidity" as two alternatives (the comorbidity part not coded); "history of diabetes,
  HbA1c ≥ 6.5 % or glucose-lowering treatment" as three alternatives (type 1 and other agents
  not coded); liver tests in multiples of the upper limit of normal left without rules.
- NCT07817290: prediabetes or type 2 diabetes added before HbA1c < 7 %; "age under 65" as
  at most 64; known cardiovascular disease as history of myocardial infarction, coronary
  bypass or stroke (not exhaustive); "history"/"previous" conditions count resolved episodes.
