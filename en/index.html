  6. Step 6 of 10

    Later, a clinical trial looks for patients.

    Before a new medicine reaches the pharmacy, it is tried on volunteers: that is a clinical trial. Each trial looks for people with precise characteristics, and to find them someone reads the records one by one. This is a real trial, from the public registry ClinicalTrials.gov: “Obesity, without diabetes”. It has 8 criteria: who can take part and who cannot. Imagine Giulia's hospital takes part: the program reads every record, Giulia's included. It is not looking for Giulia: it looks for whoever meets the requirements.

    The trial is real and public; no link with whoever runs it.

    Criteria as rules from the ClinicalTrials.gov registry to typed rules in disjunctive normal form, reviewed by hand: 3 of 8 criteria with rules src/cn2f/trials/criteria.py

  9. Step 9 of 10

    In the end, a person decides.

    “Needs review”: no criterion excludes Giulia; 6 must be checked by a person. Of 15 people, Giulia is number 3. None of her letters gives her weight, yet the synthetic record Giulia was made from says obesity: she would be a candidate. That is why the program does not drop her and asks a person to look.

    The coordinator chooses whether to go on. Eligibility is always decided by the investigator. We know it only because the data are invented: in a real hospital nobody would know before asking.

    Recorded decision the coordinator's confirm or set-aside in the hash-chained audit; never “eligible”: the investigator decides src/cn2f/prescreen/decisions.py

  10. Step 10 of 10

    What this shows.

    The same path was measured on hundreds of cases, and every number has its interval in the reports.

    Measurement cluster-bootstrap intervals, an LLM judge calibrated on physicians, a regression gate in make eval src/cn2f/prescreen/report.py

    • Of 100 facts the artificial intelligence pulls out, 81 are right; of 100 facts in the reports, it finds 86.
    • Of 100 facts recorded on their own, about 92 are right.
    • Picking at random one eligible and one non-eligible patient, the eligible one ranks higher 77 times out of 100 (a tie counting as half), in English as in Italian.
    • The weak point: about one eligible patient in 4 (25% in English, 22% in Italian) ends up “likely excluded”. That is why a person decides.
    • Instructions hidden in trial text: in the 36 cases where the model saw them, 0 verdicts moved to “likely eligible”.
    • All the project's model calls: about 3.00 USD.

    What was built, technically

    • Structured extraction from Italian clinical text with small LLMs: output bound to a JSON Schema, a verbatim quote required for every item src/cn2f/extract/schema.py
    • Pseudonymisation before any external call (Presidio + spaCy Italian, Italian recognisers such as the fiscal code); the re-identification map stays local src/cn2f/pseudo/engine.py
    • A single gateway to the models (OpenRouter): outbound personal-data guard, prompt-injection detector, spend cap, on-disk cache, hash-chained audit log src/cn2f/trust/gateway.py
    • Confidence scoring calibrated on verifiable signals (logistic regression on five signals, threshold chosen by bootstrap) with a human review queue src/cn2f/score/model.py
    • Mapping to HL7 FHIR R4 (HAPI FHIR, $validate) and terminology coding; AI transparency following HL7 AI Transparency on FHIR (AIAST label, confidence, Provenance), validated against the HL7 profiles src/cn2f/fhir/client.py
    • An MCP server (official Python SDK 2.x) exposing the FHIR record to agents src/cn2f/mcp_server/server.py
    • Agentic pre-screening integration for clinical trials: ClinicalTrials.gov criteria as typed rules, local BM25 retrieval, one call, a verifier in code for every citation, one correction src/cn2f/prescreen/run.py
    • Coordinator memory: typed rules per trial, quarantine for anything not from a person, expiry, forgetting proven on the bytes, compared with the rule given as text to a model src/cn2f/prescreen/memory.py
    • A System One model (TypeSafe Jev) as a fast first stage: typed decisions through the same gateway, measured on TREC 2022 against the agent, thresholds chosen on held-out topics; in the demo it orders within the same outcome and excludes nobody src/cn2f/prescreen/jev_eval.py
    • A panel of three System One models (Jev, Microsoft-Decision-1, Cloudflare Clef) as a second opinion per criterion, against physician labels, with robustness checks src/cn2f/prescreen/panel_eval.py
    • Evaluation on three tracks (TREC 2022 English and Italian, physician labels, the whole chain) with bootstrap intervals, a calibrated LLM judge and a regression gate in make eval src/cn2f/prescreen/evaluate.py
    • Security benchmarks: prompt injection in notes, trial text and unseen paraphrases; leaks of hospital identifiers src/cn2f/security/prescreen_bench.py
    • An EU compliance dossier (AI Act, MDR, GDPR, ICH E6(R3)) whose cited evidence is checked by tests docs/compliance/README.md
    • Engineering: FastAPI + HTMX, a bilingual two-faced site, Docker Compose, a tailnet-only deployment, a static copy for GitHub Pages, the whole suite in make test; about 3.00 USD of model calls in all src/cn2f/web/app.py
