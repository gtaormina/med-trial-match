# A System One model as a first stage (TREC 2022, track A)

Generated by `python -m cn2f.prescreen.jev_eval report`. One typed yes/no question per pair ("could this patient take part in this trial?"), answered with a probability; positive class: the assessors' *eligible*. Intervals: 95%, topic-cluster bootstrap.

**Triage** (p <= 0.1 or p >= 0.9 taken as an answer, the rest left to the agent):

- en: 59% of pairs answered with confidence, 93% of them right; eligible patients it would clear as not eligible: 13 of 100.
- it: 61% of pairs answered with confidence, 92% of them right; eligible patients it would clear as not eligible: 15 of 100.

**Thresholds chosen on held-out topics.** The triage numbers above take p <= 0.1 as a
'no' on the same pairs they are counted on. Here the threshold is chosen on half of
the topics (the lowest p of any eligible patient there: nobody lost on that half) and
applied to the other half, with Platt scaling fitted on the same half; 200 random
splits, every pair held out once per split. Median [5th–95th percentile]:

| language | pairs a cascade would skip | eligible lost (of 100) | ECE raw | ECE after Platt |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| en | 23% [23%–33%] | 0.0 [0.0–4.0] | 0.121 | 0.050 [0.032–0.065] |
| it | 21% [19%–26%] | 1.0 [1.0–3.0] | 0.129 | 0.056 [0.042–0.069] |

- Jev returns a probability and no evidence: it can order and triage pairs, never
  stand in for the agent, whose every answer cites the record and is checked by code.
- One question per pair, the registry text as given, no tuning: a first look, not a
  benchmark. Public synthetic TREC cases; the Italian texts are machine translations.
- The TREC 2022 topics and trials are public: they may be in the model's training data,
  which would flatter it (the same caveat holds for the agent's models).
- The agent's score is coarse (verdict tier plus the share of criteria met), so its AUC
  ranks with many ties; Jev's probability is continuous. Like for like on the pairs,
  not on the scale.
- In triage the cost of a confident 'no' is a lost eligible patient: that is the number
  to watch before letting a first stage drop anyone.
- The OpenRouter endpoint does not state its data policy; the texts are synthetic and
  pass the outbound guard like every model call.
