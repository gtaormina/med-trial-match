**Second opinion** (pre-registered rule: the panel speaks when all three give the same met or not met with probability >= 0.8). A *conflict*: the agent decided, the panel says the opposite. A *miss*: the agent could not decide, the panel can. Flags send a criterion to review; they never change an answer.

- The panel gives probabilities, never evidence: it can point a reviewer at a criterion, never answer it. The agent's answers stay the ones that cite the record.
- The physicians' convention reads silence as absence; the models were told, like the agent, that what the note does not mention is unknown. Silent rows are left out.
- One physician label per row, 53 patients: small numbers, wide intervals.
- The TrialGPT notes and criteria are public: they may be in the models' training data.
- The Decisions API documentation states no data policy; the texts are public synthetic cases and pass the outbound guard like every model call.
